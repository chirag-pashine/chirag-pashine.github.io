Privacy Policy — Japa & Sadhana Counter

Last updated: 2026-10-24

Japa & Sadhana Counter ("the app", package com.apksoftware.japamala) is a digital chanting counter and sadhana journal. We respect your privacy.

Data the app stores

All your data - chanting counts, rounds, history, mantras, sankalpas, the sadhana journal (wake-up and sleep times, reading, hearing, temple, seva, Ekadashi, checklist, notes), the optional profile (name, phone, temple, mentor) and settings - is stored only on your device. It is never uploaded to any server. There is no account or login. Backups and reports (PDF, CSV, text messages) are files or messages that you create and share yourself.

Microphone

If you turn on Count by voice, the app listens through the microphone only while that mode is on and the chanting screen is open. Sound is analysed in memory on your device to recognise your mantra. To learn your mantra, the app keeps numeric voice features of a few of your recitations on the device (no audio). Audio is not recorded or sent anywhere. Only if you turn on the optional Record a voice sample (off by default and after every restart), the last 3 minutes are kept in memory so that you can share them yourself with Share voice sample. You can deny or revoke microphone access at any time; the rest of the app works without it.

Notifications and sounds

The app schedules a single local daily reminder on your device if you enable it. No push notification service is used. The optional Japa shield only mutes notification sounds (and optionally the ringtone) on your phone while you chant and restores them afterwards; the app does not read your notifications.

Advertising

The app shows one banner ad using Google AdMob. Google may collect and process device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location (from the IP address), and interaction/diagnostic information to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Where required (e.g. in the EEA/UK), the app asks for your consent with Google's User Messaging Platform, and you can change your choice in Settings > Ad privacy choices. Without consent, non-personalized ads are shown. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Use Settings > Reset all data, or uninstall the app.

Contact

Please use the developer e-mail address shown on the app's Google Play page.
